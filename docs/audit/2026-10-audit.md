## 1. Summary

Every cost at 6.9 kVA or below is 7 to 12% too high because VAT is a flat 23% (ACC-1). And the data is 10 months old while the footer says "atualizado mensalmente" (PIPE-2).

### Critical

#### ACC-1. Flat 23% VAT on every component, and no DGEG fee, overstate the monthly cost by 7 to 12% at 6.9 kVA or below
- **Severity:** critical. **Area:** ACC.
- **Evidence:** `js/calculator.js:129-132` computes `(TF*30 + energy + IEC + 2.85) * 1.23`, with VAT 1.23 from `js/config.js:6`. `docs/DATA_MODEL.mdc:133, :199-203` say the same. ADR-009 (`docs/DECISIONS.mdc:316-351`) claims ±2%, and `sobre.html:169` says "IVA a 23%". The law in force is different:
  - Lei 38/2024, verba 2.38: 6% on energy, fixed components excluded, at 6.90 kVA or below, on the first 200 kWh per 30 days (300 kWh for households of 5 or more). In force since 2025-01-01.
  - DL 60/2019, verba 2.33: 6% on the network-access fixed component at 3.45 kVA or below.
  - CAV is taxed at 6%. IEC and DGEG are taxed at 23%.
  
  `$SCRATCH/acc/harness.mjs` on real offers (app vs the rules):

  | Case | App | Rules | Difference |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3.45 kVA, 200 kWh | 45.28 | 40.33 | +12.3% |
  | 6.9 kVA bi-hourly, 350 kWh | 78.75 | 73.44 | +7.2% |
  | 2.3 kVA, 100 kWh | 26.20 | 23.33 | +12.3% |
  | 10.35 kVA | 120.28 | 119.88 | +0.3% |

  The rule model reproduces ERSE's own Exemplo 3 to the cent: 63.10 EUR, where the app gives 66.30.
- **Impact:** every typical household (6.9 kVA or below) sees a "Custo mensal" that is 3 to 5 EUR a month too high, and the about page states a wrong rule. The winner changes in only 6 of 144 profiles, each time by cents, so the harm is in the numbers shown.
- **Fix:** tax each component separately in `calculateMonthlyCost`:
  - the energy split at min(1, 200/kWh) between 6% and 23%, prorated by period;
  - the fixed term at 23%, with 6% on the TAR share at 3.45 kVA or below (see owner question 5);
  - IEC and DGEG at 23%, CAV at 6%;
  - add DGEG 0.07 EUR to `js/config.js`.
  
  In the same commit, update DATA_MODEL, ADR-009 and `sobre.html`, and pin ERSE's example values in the selftest.
- **Effort:** M. **Complexity cost:** 2 or 3 constants and about 15 lines, plus the test.
- **Confidence:** verified. **Source:** Lei 38/2024 https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Lei_38_2024.pdf ; DL 60/2019 https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Decreto_Lei_60_2019.pdf ; ERSE simulator manual https://www.erse.pt/media/5cen41xv/manual-simulador_jun26.pdf pp.19-20 ; ERSExplica IVA https://www.erse.pt/media/yodok3zt/ersexplica_aplica%C3%A7%C3%A3o-do-iva.pdf (all read 2026-10-20).
- **Verification:** confirmed by verification. Both laws were re-fetched from portaldasfinancas. The verifier's own model (`$SCRATCH/verify/vat.mjs`) gives the same deltas. The ERSE PDFs could not be re-fetched (TLS error), and the DGEG amount was not checked against a source.

#### ACC-2. Estimating kWh from euros counts the fixed term twice, so the "Poupas" figure is about double
- **Severity:** critical. **Area:** ACC.
- **Evidence:** `js/calculator.js:46-51` builds the price per kWh as TF*30/200 + TV, and `:76-77` then subtracts the median fixed term again. The round-trip test `$SCRATCH/acc/roundtrip.mjs` takes 200 kWh to 55.41 EUR, and back to 144 kWh. For an 80 EUR bill the app estimates 234 kWh, where the forward formula inverted gives 313 kWh:

  | Bill | Savings shown (month) | Savings without the double count |
  |---|---|---|
  | 40 EUR | 12.72 | 7.68 |
  | 80 EUR | 25.72 | 11.65 |
  | 120 EUR | 38.95 | 17.47 |

  `docs/DATA_MODEL.mdc:219-234` documents a version without the double count.
- **Impact:** estimate mode is the default flow, and its savings figure, the product's main loss-framing number, is about 2.2x what the app's own model supports. The "Consumo estimado" shown is too low.
- **Fix:** invert `calculateMonthlyCost` numerically (bisect kWh until the cost equals the bill on the median offer), and add the round trip as a selftest case.
- **Effort:** S. **Complexity cost:** a 20-line bisection, or a one-line change. No new concepts.
- **Confidence:** verified. **Source:** code and harness; diagnosing-bugs lens.
- **Verification:** confirmed by verification. An independent bisection in `$SCRATCH/verify/v.mjs` matches every figure.

## 5. Tariff switching

This is condensed from area SWITCH; all sources were read 2026-10-20.

**Recommendation.** Build only a better **guided self-switch**: free, no data collected, no backend. Paid switching (affiliate or lead) is **test first**. Filing switches directly is **closed**.

**How a switch works in Portugal:**
- The consumer chooses. The new supplier is the point of contact and does everything, and the switch is free: ERSE, "Contratação e mudança de comercializador", Nov 2024, https://www.erse.pt/media/j4dhfebu/erseforma_contrata%C3%A7%C3%A3omudanca_nov2024.pdf ; DL 15/2022 art. 152(4) (2022 PDF copy, https://ordemdosengenheiros.pt/fotos/especialidades/0000300185_19938915836571aac8a132c.pdf ; the consolidated DRE text was not readable).
- The maximum is 3 weeks, and the final bill from the old supplier comes within 6 weeks: ERSE guide, and ERSE Síntese Mensal Jun 2026, https://www.erse.pt/media/pt0obw32/202606_ele_rel_ml.pdf . The "about 5 working days" figure comes only from secondary sources.
- EU Directive 2019/944 art. 12 asks for 24-hour technical switching "by no later than 2026", https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2019/944/article/12/adopted . Whether Portugal has implemented it was not verified; ERSE's June 2026 summary still says 3 weeks.
- Switches go through OLMC/ADENE's GPMC platform, which is for suppliers and distributors only, https://olmc.adene.pt/atividade/ . The consumer must confirm, and the supplier must keep auditable express authorisation, under ERSE's acquisition sanctions (press release of 26 Nov 2024).
- Lock-in is capped at 12 months, and the fee cannot exceed direct losses (ERSE guide).
- Volume: 69,674 residential switches within the free market in June 2026, out of about 5.78 M residential free-market customers.

**Money and models:**
- No supplier has a public API, and no affiliate programme was found.
- Repsol pays a 30 EUR consumer referral per contract (https://www.repsol.pt/particulares/casa/eletricidade-gas/recomendar-repsol-amigo/), a floor on a contract's value to a supplier.
- Selectra is paid per contract and reserves the right to put a partner offer first (https://selectra.pt/metodologia). ComparaJá takes a commission (https://www.comparaja.pt/sobre-nos).
- The ERSE simulator and Poupa Energia are public and free, and DL 15/2022 art. 184(2) requires ERSE's tool.
- Directive art. 14(1) asks a trustworthy comparator to disclose its financing and show the time of the last update (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2019/944/article/14/adopted). GuessWatts fails the "last update shown" part today (PIPE-2).
